# layout

`sky130_lvds_tx_driver_pad.gds` is generated by `gen_driver_pad.py`; do not
edit the GDS by hand.

```
source .venv/bin/activate && export PDK_ROOT=~/.ciel PDK=sky130A
klayout -b -r layout/gen_driver_pad.py        # rewrites the GDS
sh verification/signoff/check-driver-pad-gds.sh   # fresh == committed, slot fit
```

The output is byte-identical across runs (no timestamps; `klt gen` is
deterministic). A missing stock I/O GDS fails loudly.

## What is drawn (issue #22 stages 1-3; driver routed in issue #48)

- `sky130_lvds_tx_pad_cell`: the PDK's `sky130_ef_io__analog_noesd_pad` frame
  (75 x 200 um, read from the pinned PDK, DR-001) plus the pad-to-VSSIO
  grounded-gate ESD NMOS (`klt gen esd_device`: 5 fingers x 17.2 um, L 0.55,
  the 86 um total of `pad_cell.sch`'s `XMESD`).
- `sky130_lvds_tx_pad_pair`: two pad cells at the 75 um pitch (PADP, PADN),
  150 x 200 um.
- `sky130_lvds_tx_driver`: the `design/driver/driver.spice` devices, **placed and
  routed** (see "Routing" below): `klt gen mos_array` per device (thick-oxide ones
  with `voltage_flavor=hvi`, plus a guard ring for the bulk tie and `gate_contact`),
  `klt gen res_array` flavor `high` for `XRS1/2`. The netlist is flattened, so there
  is no `drv_sel` / `drv_slice` cell level: each device is a leaf cell named
  `<parent>_<device>` (`drv_slice_s11_XMPS`, `drv_slice_s1_XMSPA` x3, `drv_sel_XMP_sb`
  x3, `drv_XMNB1`, ...) placed directly in `sky130_lvds_tx_driver`. Unit gates carry
  `<device>.U<i>_G` labels, so `m` = the number of `_G` labels. Widths are per
  finger = card `W` / `nf` (the card `W` is the total, as the `ad`/`as` values and
  `klt lvs` assume); the stage-2 placement used `W` per finger, which doubled every
  `nf = 2` device and quadrupled the `nf = 4` ones.
- `sky130_lvds_tx_ddr_mux` (18 devices) and `sky130_lvds_tx_predriver` (50
  devices): placement only, one `klt gen mos_array` cell per device, named
  `ddr_mux_<device>` / `predriver_<device>`. The device lists are parsed from
  `design/ddr_mux/ddr_mux.spice` and `design/predriver/predriver.spice` (all
  1.8 V flavour), so the generator cannot drift from the netlists.
- `sky130_lvds_tx_driver_pad`: the pair, then the driver, `ddr_mux` and
  `predriver` beside it (541.5 x 200.9 um in all; only the driver is routed).

Driver area: 148.9 x 183.3 um (about 27,280 um^2) against one 75 x 200 um slot
(15,000 um^2) or the two-slot pair (30,000 um^2). That is the routed figure: it
includes a bulk guard ring on every device and 12.2 um routing channels between
the device rows, so it is not comparable with the 13,560 um^2 of placed device
area reported before routing. It does not drop into a single pad slot, and fits
the two-slot pair only if the pair's bus rails are not subtracted, which they are
not here. It sits outside the pad cells, which keep their 75 x 200 um footprint.
P1/P3 are PROPOSED and unchanged; this is a report, not a spec edit.

Stage 3 area: `ddr_mux` 63.9 x 4.8 um (about 304 um^2) and `predriver` 148.7 x
18.8 um (about 2,793 um^2). The three analog blocks together are about 16,655
um^2 against one 75 x 200 um slot (15,000 um^2), an overflow of roughly 1,655
um^2 (11%) as shelf-packed, and well inside the two-slot pair (30,000 um^2).
Shelf packing is not a floorplan and no bus rails are subtracted, so this is a
report of the placed device area, not a fit verdict. P1/P3 are unchanged.

## Routing (issue #48)

`sky130_lvds_tx_driver` is built from `design/driver/driver.spice` by
`gen_driver_pad.py`, with no hand-drawn geometry:

1. The netlist is flattened (`drv_sel`, `drv_slice SCALE=n`) into 89 leaf devices
   with flat net names; the top-level `driver` ports become the pins.
2. Each leaf gets, per terminal, a stack at every unit pin (li1, `mcon`, met1,
   `via`, met2), a met2 stub, and one met3 rail: source and bulk rails below the
   cell, drain and gate rails above it. The drain stub runs down instead of up
   when a short-L gate pin sits within 0.45 um of it. The bulk tie is the
   generator's guard ring (`TAP_W`).
3. Leaves are shelf-packed on a 0.68 um grid (3.4 um between leaves in a row,
   12.2 um between rows) so every rail sits on a grid row.
4. A maze router (met3 horizontal, met4 vertical, `via3`) joins the rails of each
   net, shortest nets first and deterministically; a net that cannot be routed
   stops the run. Pin names are met3 labels (70/5) on a rail of the net.

The router wire is 0.38 um on met3 and 0.34 um on met4 and was not sized for DC
current or electromigration: the supply and `OUTP`/`OUTN` nets are as thin as a
signal. That is a known limit, not a claim.

Checked on the routed layout (`klt lvs` / `klt drc`, `sky130` deck):

- **LVS** (`verification/signoff/lvs/item-4-analog.request.json`, now flattened,
  `combine_devices` and `compare_mos_flavour`): 89 devices and 50 nets on each
  side, the 18 pins present on both. Against the committed `driver.spice` the
  report is still `mismatch`: 81 of 89 devices and 37 of 50 nets pair, and the
  other 8 devices are the `SCALE=11` slice, whose `m={SCALE}` the converter does
  not evaluate (2AMLogic/klayout-tools#4406). With `{SCALE}` substituted into a
  derived netlist (`verification/signoff/lvs/expand-scale.py`) the same layout
  gives `match`: 89/89 devices, 50/50 nets, 18/18 pins, widths and lengths
  compared. Warnings remain: `device.body_unverified` (8 PMOS bodies sit on
  internal, unlabeled well nets), `res_high_po` geometry not compared. Both
  reports are in `verification/signoff/evidence/` (`item-4-lvs-analog-r2*.json`).
- **Device recognition**: `compare_mos_flavour` separates `g5v0d10v5` from `01v8`;
  swapping one thick-oxide reference card to `nfet_01v8` produces a `device.class`
  mismatch, so the deck does tell them apart. The ESD clamp, the `D1` diode and
  the pad frame are not in this compare (the top is the driver cell), so their
  recognition is still not established.
- **DRC** (`klt drc --deck sky130 --top sky130_lvds_tx_driver_pad`): 486
  violations, the same per-rule counts as `item-3-drc-analog-r2.json`, all in the
  stock pad frame. The routed driver adds none. The hvi mark is extended over each
  thick-oxide PMOS guard-ring well (`nwell.enclosed.hvi.1` fired 43 times without it).

## Not drawn yet (deferred)

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| `OUTP` / `OUTN` to the pad cells, the clamp to `P_CORE` / VSSIO | the driver sits beside the pad pair and its pins are labels only; the pad seam needs a via stack and DRC feedback (#24) |
| `D1` (`diode_pw2nd_11v0`) | `klt gen` has no diode generator |
| `XMESD` as `nfet_g5v0d10v5` | `esd_device` draws no thick-oxide / ESD marker on sky130, so the clamp is geometry only |
| Routing of `ddr_mux` and `predriver` | still placement only (stage 3); they also still use `W` per finger, so every `nf = 2` device is drawn twice as wide as the netlist |
| Current-sized supply and output wiring | the router uses one wire width |

T1 item 2 (analog) is claimed as **provenance only**: the committed GDS is
reproducibly generated (`verification/signoff/check-driver-pad-gds.sh`), and
the envelope cited as `2.analog` in `manifests/sky130-lvds-tx.json` was written
for the placement-only layout. This change regenerates the GDS, so that item-2
evidence is stale against the committed file; the manifest and its evidence are
untouched here (#25). Neither DRC nor LVS is claimed clean.

## DRC of the stock pad frame (issue #44)

`klt drc --deck sky130 --top sky130_lvds_tx_driver_pad` reports 486 `met4`/`met5`
violations (`verification/signoff/evidence/item-3-drc-analog-r2.json`; the earlier
`item-3-drc-analog.json` is kept, append-only). Every one has `source_cell` in the stock
frame, and **none is drawn here**: the same counts reproduce on the unmodified PDK cells
with none of this repo's geometry present (`sh verification/signoff/check-pad-frame-drc.sh`,
`local_only`, asserts the numbers below). Nothing was waived and neither the deck nor the
spec was changed; the layout is unchanged, so the analog row stays `unmet`.

Per stock `sky130_ef_io__analog_noesd_pad` (257; the pair is two of them), rule by rule.
"Native" is the foundry's own `sky130A_mr.drc` (`klt drc --engine klayout`) on the same
cell: it flags 241 (the 5 met4/met5 rules below plus 6 `m1.3ab` on met1, out of this
issue's scope).

| Curated rule | Count | Native rule, count | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| `met4.space.1` | 108 | `m4.2`, 108 | Real by the rule as written, in foundry geometry. Not placement. |
| `met4.enclosing.via3.1` | 80 | `m4.3`, 80 | Same. |
| `met4.width.1` | 20 | `m4.1`, 20 | Same (slivers, e.g. 25 nm x 678 nm). |
| `met4.area.1` | 1 | `m4.4`, 1 | Same. |
| `met4.holes_area.1` | 20 | none (`sky130A_mr.drc` has no met4 hole-area rule) | Not established: only the curated deck checks it; the holes are 110 x 80 and 320 x 80 nm. Neither "real" nor "false positive" is claimed. |
| `met5.width.1` | 26 | `m5.1`, 26 | Edge artefact of the stock cell, real as placed: all 26 touch the cell's left or right edge (stripes 0.97-1.27 um wide, rule 1.6 um). In a row of 5 abutted cells they appear only at the two row ends (14 + 14 markers), so interior edges close. Our pair has two free ends. |
| `met5.area.1` | 2 | none flagged | Same edge stripes (0.97 x 3.25 um = 3.1 um^2, rule 4.0 um^2); the native `m5.4` does not flag them and why is not traced. |

The met4 findings scale with cell count (5 abutted cells: 400 `via3` enclosure, 540 spacing,
100 width, 100 holes, 5 area), are interior to `sky130_ef_io__simple_pad_and_busses`, and
no area-ID marker in the stream covers them, so nothing in the geometry marks them exempt.
So they are not false positives of the curated deck: the foundry deck agrees on 4 of the 5
met4 groups. Whether the foundry waives these in its I/O library is not documented in the
PDK files read here and is **not established**; that is a ruling for the operator, since
the only route to a `met` analog row is a waiver of foundry-supplied geometry, which this
repo does not add on its own.
